My Top 5 Video Games

These are my five favorite video games, ranked in order.

  1. The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind

    Morrowind is an open-ended RPG where you can create your own character and explore a fantasy world. You can choose to follow the main story or head off on your own and find your own places to explore.

  2. Cyberpunk 2077

    Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world RPG set in a Cyberpunk/dystopian city called Night City. Its futuristic setting revolves around power, advanced technology, and people heavily modifying their bodies with cybernetic upgrades.

  3. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

    The Witcher 3 follows a character named Geralt of Rivia who is a monster hunter searching for his adopted daughter, Ciri. The game takes place in a dark fantasy world with quests where your decisions can have consequences and affect how events turn out.

  4. Red Dead Redemption 2

    Red Dead Redemption 2 follows Arthur Morgan who is an outlaw traveling with the Van der Linde gang in 1899 towards the end of the Wild West era. The story primarily deals with the survival of the gang as pressure from law enforcement and internal issues within the gang threaten their way of life.

  5. Dishonored

    Dishonored is a stealth/action game that combines both stealth and the main character's supernatural abilities. You can use different powers, weapons, and items to approach missions quietly or fight your way through. Your actions throughout the game affect the outcome of the story.
