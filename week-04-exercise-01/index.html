A Jiu-Jitsu Gym on Mars

I thought the idea of NASA opening a martial arts gym on Mars would be somewhat entertaining, and it was very easy to edit the title in DevTools. These changes obviously did not make changes that others would be able to see.

What I "Vandalized"

I changed the NASA headline from "Seeking Next Generation of Explorers" to "NASA Opens the Very First Jiu-Jitsu Gym on Mars." In the Styles panel, I set the color to dark green and background-color to a tannish gold so the headline would stand out against the astronaut photo.
